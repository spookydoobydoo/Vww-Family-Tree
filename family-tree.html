How the manual layout works

Move each cat into the position you want. A small orange diamond represents one set of parents and their shared children. Drag that diamond to adjust where the branch joins. Multiple ex-mates can have separate diamonds, so each child has the right parents.

A niece or nephew is connected as the child of a selected sibling; the reverse aunt/uncle relationship is calculated automatically. A partner relationship alone does not make someone a parent. Use Connect Existing to add that relationship.
